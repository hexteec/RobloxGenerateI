This Privacy Policy explains how RoPeak ("we", "us", the "Service") collects, uses, and protects data in connection with your use of our icon, thumbnail, 3D model, and Lua script generation platform for Roblox creators. By using RoPeak, you agree to the practices described below.

2.How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the Service: generating images, 3D models, Lua scripts, and maintaining your chat history.
  • To manage your token balance and process purchases.
  • To display statistics for the games/Roblox accounts you add as "Live Stats" widgets.
  • To improve and secure the Service and to prevent abuse.
  • To contact you about your account or support requests.

3.AI-Generated Content

Your prompts and uploaded images (e.g. reference photos, UI screenshots) may be sent to third-party AI model providers in order to generate an image, 3D model, or code. We do not use your private prompts to train our own models without your consent.

4.Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may only be shared with:

  • infrastructure providers (Firebase/Google) — authentication and database services;
  • our payment processor (Stripe) — to process token purchases;
  • generative AI model providers — only to the extent needed to generate content you requested;
  • if you publish a model to the Community Store — its thumbnail, title, and description become publicly visible.

5.Roblox Account Linking

If you choose to link your Roblox account, we only retrieve publicly available data (username, avatar, game stats) needed to display "Live Stats" widgets. You can disconnect this link at any time.

6.Data Retention & Security

We retain your data for as long as you maintain an active account, unless you request deletion sooner. We apply standard security measures (encrypted connections, token-based authentication), though no method of data transmission is ever 100% secure.

7.Your Rights

  • The right to access your data and chat history.
  • The right to correct or delete your account and associated data.
  • The right to withdraw consent to processing at any time.
  • The right to data portability, where technically feasible.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8.Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use essential cookies/local storage to maintain your login session and remember your preferences (e.g. selected theme and interface language).

9.Children

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. If we learn that we have collected data from someone under this age without parental consent, we will delete it.

10.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via an in-app notice or email.

11.Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, reach out to us — contact details are available on the Service's homepage or in your account settings.
